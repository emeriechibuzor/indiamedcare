Before you order

Questions, answered plainly

Everything customers ask us most — delivery, payments and what happens if something goes wrong.

How long does delivery take?

Orders are dispatched within 24 hours of payment verification. Metro cities usually receive within 2–4 working days; the rest of India within 4–7 working days. You get a tracking link as soon as the courier scans the parcel.

How do I pay with Bitcoin or USDT?

Choose crypto at checkout, copy the wallet address for the coin and network shown, send the exact total, then upload a screenshot of the transaction. We verify it and confirm by email or WhatsApp.

Are the medicines genuine?

Yes. We source only from licensed distributors, and every batch number and expiry date is checked against the invoice before dispatch. If you ever receive something you doubt, we replace or refund it in full.

Do I need a prescription?

Over-the-counter items, supplements and personal care need no prescription. For scheduled medicines we may ask for a valid prescription from a registered practitioner before dispatch, as required by Indian law.

What if my order arrives damaged or wrong?

Message us on WhatsApp within 48 hours with a photo of the item and the packaging. We arrange a replacement or a refund to your wallet — you do not pay return shipping for our mistakes.

Can I track my order?

Every order gets a tracking number once it is handed to the courier. You can also open your account page to see order status, or ask us on WhatsApp with your order number.
